Name this window

Shown in the "Send link to window" menu. Left empty, the window is listed by the title of its active tab. Names last until Edge is closed, because the window ids they belong to do not outlive it either.

Both are saved together by the button below. Alt-click any link to send it to that window without opening the menu; Alt+Shift-click does the opposite of your defaults — background if they switch, switch if they do not.
